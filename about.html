About the maker

A curious mind with a practical streak.

I’m studying interaction design and learning to turn messy questions into simple, kind experiences.

My north star

Good design helps someone do what they came to do, then gets out of the way. I like working at the intersection of words, visual systems, and front-end code.

Outside a browser, you’ll find me collecting overheard phrases, making pour-over coffee, or walking with a camera and no route.

“The best interface is an invitation, not an instruction manual.”

— a principle I’m still learning to practice

How I work

Make, test, explain, repeat.

  1. 01

    Start with the person

    I write down what someone needs to know or do before I choose a colour or component.

  2. 02

    Build a small version

    A working rough draft reveals more than a perfect plan. I use HTML and CSS as thinking tools.

  3. 03

    Keep a visible trail

    Each week gets a meaningful commit: a decision, a fix, or a lesson I can explain later.
